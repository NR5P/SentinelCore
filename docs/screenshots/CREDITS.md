# Screenshot credits

from free stock videos on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use and modify, no attribution
required; credited here anyway). They were cropped, colour-graded to look like security-camera
footage, and given a burned-in camera name and clock. None of it comes from a real home camera.
The people shown are actors in stock footage; the event descriptions are written for the demo.
