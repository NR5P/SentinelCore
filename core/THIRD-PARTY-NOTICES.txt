SentinelCore - Third-Party Notices
==================================

SentinelCore redistributes the components listed below. Each is used under the license
shown, and the required notices are reproduced here.

This file covers what actually ships inside a SentinelCore release archive. Components
that SentinelCore merely talks to, and that the operator installs themselves through
their own package manager - notably ffmpeg, go2rtc and Ollama - are not redistributed
and are not covered here; they remain under their own licenses as installed.

Two models are downloaded on first use rather than shipped, and so are also not
redistributed by us: RF-DETR Nano (fast local detection) and CLIP (semantic search).
